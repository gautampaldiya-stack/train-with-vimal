  2. 2Grip slightly wider than shoulder width and unrack with straight arms.
  3. 3Lower the bar to the mid chest in about two seconds, elbows at roughly 45°.
  4. 4Press up and slightly back until the arms are straight.
  • Squeeze the bar and keep the wrists stacked over the elbows.
  • Keep the hips on the bench; drive through the feet.
  • Touch the chest, do not bounce.
