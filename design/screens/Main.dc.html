Sidebar
The left rail on every web page. 240 wide, white, items 34 high with radius 8; hover and the active item both use #FAFAFA with a 1px line, and the active item's icon turns accent blue. Group labels (Build, Engage, Workspace) are uppercase 11. A count chip sits at the right end of an item that is waiting on you (Approvals). The round 28 chevron on the panel edge collapses the rail to 68 icons-only and shows only while the pointer is on the rail; in the collapsed rail the labels become tooltips, group labels become a 1px divider, and the count moves to a corner badge. Width does not animate. The brand block shows the logo, the workspace name and the role line — “Owner workspace” for admin, “Trainer workspace” for a trainer. On a phone there is no rail: a 5-tab bottom bar takes its place and everything else moves to the More page.

Rules
Order
Admin: Dashboard, Clients, Approvals, Trainers, Calendar, Activity log · Build: Programs, Exercises, Nutrition & foods, Habits & supplements, Forms & check-ins, Content library · Engage: Automations, Emails & alerts · Workspace: Settings.
Trainer workspace
Dashboard, My clients, Check-ins, Calendar · Library: Programs, Exercises, Nutrition & foods, Habits & supplements, Content library · You: My profile. Same parts, shorter list, no Approvals, Trainers, Automations, Emails or Settings.
Phone
Admin tabs: Home, Clients, Approvals (badge), Calendar, More. Trainer tabs: Home, Clients, Check-ins, Calendar, More. The More page repeats the same groups as rows, with the account card on top and Log out at the bottom.
Never
No scrollbar in the rail, no second level of nesting, no page opening in the rail itself, and the rail never scrolls away from the content panel.
Page header
Title on the left, actions always on the right in one line. The title shrinks with the width (28 desktop → 20 phone) and wraps to 2 lines max. Under ~760px the outline buttons become icon-only (tooltip keeps the name); on a phone every button is icon-only.

Toast
The one line that says a change went through, so a saved change never needs a modal. It slides up, holds about 4 seconds, and goes on its own; Undo keeps it open while the pointer is on it. Title says what happened in the past tense, the grey second line gives the detail, Undo sits before the close ×, and only one toast shows at a time — a second change replaces the first. A toast never carries the only copy of something the person must read, and an error that needs a decision is a modal, not a toast.
