1 · Eating out is fine

One meal does not undo a week

Fat loss comes from what you do most days. A dinner out is one meal out of about 28 in a week. Plan it, enjoy it, log it, and go back to your plan at the next meal.

1

Look at the menu before you go and pick your order at home.

2

Eat a normal, protein-rich lunch. Don’t arrive starving.

3

Log the meal the same night. A rough log beats no log.

2 · Rules of thumb

Six rules that work in any restaurant

You don’t need to know the calories of every dish. These six habits get you most of the way.

Start with protein

Order a tikka, a grilled fish, an egg dish or a dal first. Protein fills you up, so you eat less of everything else.

Grilled over fried

Tandoori, grilled, roasted and steamed are your friends. Fried, crispy and “golden” add 150–300 kcal a plate.

Sauces and dressings on the side

Ask for them on the side and use a spoon, not the whole bowl. Butter and cream sauces are where calories hide.

One carb, not three

Pick rice or roti or naan. Two rotis or one small bowl of rice is plenty with a protein and a vegetable.

Half a plate of vegetables

Salad, raita, a dry sabzi or a clear soup. They make the plate big without making it heavy.

Stop at comfortably full

Put the spoon down for two minutes halfway through. If you are still hungry after that, eat more vegetables.

3 · Reading an Indian menu

The words on the menu tell you a lot

Most Indian menus use the same words for how a dish is cooked. Learn these and you can order anywhere.

Pick often

  • Tandoori
  • Tikka
  • Grilled / roasted
  • Dal tadka
  • Chana masala
  • Raita, salad
  • Clear soup

Sometimes

  • Jeera rice
  • Butter naan
  • Paneer bhurji
  • Kadai dishes
  • Biryani (half)
  • Dosa, uttapam
  • Chole bhature (share)

Rarely

  • Makhani
  • Malai
  • Shahi, korma
  • Butter masala
  • Pakora, kofta
  • “Crispy” anything
  • Extra ghee on top

Ask for: “less oil”, “no butter on top” and “gravy on the side”. Most kitchens are happy to do it, and it saves 100–200 kcal without changing the dish.

5 · Drinks and desserts

Where the hidden calories are

Drinks and desserts can add more than the meal itself. You don’t have to skip them, just pick smart.

6 · Back to plan

The next meal is a normal meal

No skipping breakfast, no extra cardio to “burn it off”. Just go back to your plan.

After a meal out

  • Log the meal in the app the same night. Pick the closest dish and an honest portion.
  • Drink 2–3 glasses of water before bed. Salty food holds water, so the scale may jump.
  • Ignore the scale for 2 days. A jump after a dinner out is water, not fat.
  • Eat your normal planned breakfast the next morning.
  • Do your next workout as planned. No punishment sessions.
